Notes on individual systems:

- **Arsenate**: Contains arsenic: toxic.
- **Barbital**: A controlled substance in many countries.
- **Borate**: Polyborate ions form above about 0.025 mol/kg total boron and are not modelled.
- **Cacodylate**: Contains arsenic: toxic.
- **Carbonate**: A closed system is assumed: CO₂ exchange with air is not modelled.
- **Hydrazine**: Toxic.
- **PIPES**: PIPES carries two sulfonate groups. Goldberg writes the buffering step as HL± = H⁺ + L⁻; here the free acid is H₂L (one sulfonic acid proton, completely dissociated in solution) and the buffering step is HL⁻ = H⁺ + L²⁻, so the free acid needs about 1.5 equivalents of base to reach its pK.
- **POPSO**: Two sulfonate groups: treated like PIPES (see its note).
- **Sulfate**: Only HSO₄⁻ = H⁺ + SO₄²⁻ is tabulated; the first proton of sulfuric acid is treated as completely dissociated.
