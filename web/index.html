Buffer recipes, corrected for temperature and ionic strength

Constants evaluated by NIST and water properties from IAPWS. The model is tested against NIST certified pH standards, and every result says how far it has been checked.

Everything is computed in this page. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

Also in the solution (mM)
Activity model

Davies is the default: it is meant for ionic strengths up to about 0.5 mol/kg. Bates–Guggenheim is the convention that defines the pH of the standards (I ≤ 0.1). The ideal solution is what most calculators assume.

Enter what is in the solution (final concentrations). Titrants count as what was added: 20 mM HCl means 20 mmol per litre of final solution.

Data: Goldberg, Kishore & Lennen, J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 31, 231 (2002); NIST SRM certificates 185i, 186-g, 187e, 191d, 922/923; IAPWS SR6-08 and R11-24; CIAAW 2024 atomic weights.
